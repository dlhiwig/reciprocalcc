Accessibility

Access to RCC’s website.

Information, contact options, and a way to report barriers.

Updated November 8, 2026

Website accessibility features

This version includes a skip-to-content link, semantic page regions and headings, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus indicators, image alternatives, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Contact options are provided as email and telephone links.

Report an accessibility barrier

Email Brandon@reciprocalcc.com or call +1 248-697-6516. Include the page URL, the task you were attempting, and the barrier you encountered. You may also describe the format or assistance that would help.

Assessment scope

Accessibility checks for this version do not establish a comprehensive conformance determination. Real-world accessibility can vary with browsers, assistive technologies, and content changes.

Product accessibility documentation

For procurement involving an RCC product or configured solution, request the Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR) applicable to that product and version. Website accessibility features do not establish Section 508 conformance for every RCC offering.
