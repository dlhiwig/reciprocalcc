Privacy

Website privacy.

How this website handles visits and inquiries.

Updated November 8, 2026

Website features

This website presents information about RCC and provides email and telephone links. It does not include an online intake form, account registration, payment processing, or a resume upload feature.

Information you choose to send

Selecting an email link opens your email application. Sending an inquiry shares your email address, message, and any attachments with RCC and the email providers involved in delivery. Include only the information needed for the inquiry.

Questions about an inquiry, including access, correction, or deletion requests, can be sent to Brandon@reciprocalcc.com. Any applicable retention or processing terms for a customer engagement should be addressed in its agreement.

Cookies, analytics, and hosting

The website’s own code does not set cookies, use browser storage, or include advertising, analytics, embedded social feeds, or third-party font requests. The hosting platform may process connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, and may use platform-level security or authentication mechanisms under its own policies.

External links

Email applications, telephone services, and external websites operate under their providers’ terms and privacy practices. Review those terms before submitting information through another service.

Scope and changes

This notice describes the website, rather than every RCC service or a customer’s deployment. Service-specific data handling and any additional notices belong in the relevant agreement. This notice should be reviewed when the website’s features or providers change.
