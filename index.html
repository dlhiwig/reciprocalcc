Engagements

Three ways to work. Nothing open-ended.

Reciprocal is not staff augmentation and not a slide factory. Each engagement has a start, a decision, and a handoff.

01

Diagnostic

Two weeks. One problem. A written decision memo the client keeps either way: what is true, what to do next, and what not to touch.

02

Build

Ninety days on a single outcome. Offer, pricing, sales motion, operating cadence, or a launch. Weekly working session. Fixed scope.

03

Retainer

Ongoing judgment for owners who already know the work. A monthly seat, a quarterly reset of the problem, and a clean way to stop.

Fit

Built for operators, not spectators.

A good fit

  • A startup with a real offer and a decision it keeps postponing.
  • A professional firm whose delivery is strong and whose business system is not.
  • A larger company with an owner on the inside and a deadline.

Not a fit

  • Hourly staff cover with no decision rights.
  • Strategy with nobody on the client side to implement it.
  • Work where success cannot be named before kickoff.
