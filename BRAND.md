# RCC Brand System

**Brand:** Reciprocal Commerce Community (RCC)  
**Approved logo direction:** Partnership Bridge  
**Primary tagline:** Partnerships That Produce Results.

## Brand idea

The Partnership Bridge symbol uses two distinct, interlocking C forms connected by a shared horizontal bridge. It represents independent people and organizations connecting through collaboration, coordinated execution, and mutual value.

## Core palette

| Token | Hex | Primary use |
|---|---|---|
| RCC Navy | `#173954` | Primary identity, headings, dark surfaces |
| RCC Teal | `#159D96` | Bridge accent, links, highlights, calls to action |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Reverse logo and clean surfaces |
| Slate | `#647482` | Secondary text and supporting elements |
| Off-white | `#F5F7F8` | Neutral page backgrounds |

## Usage rules

1. Keep the symbol and wordmark proportions intact. Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the mark.
2. Use navy and teal on light backgrounds; use white and teal on navy backgrounds.
3. Maintain clear space around the mark at least equal to the bridge bar's thickness.
4. Do not add shadows, bevels, gradients, outlines, or extra colors to the core mark.
5. Use the primary tagline consistently: **Partnerships That Produce Results.**
6. The approved brand is RCC. Do not revert to the previous standalone “Reciprocal” wordmark or copper/cream palette.

## LinkedIn specifications

The LinkedIn Pages cover asset is exported at **4200 x 700 px**, following LinkedIn's current recommended Company Page cover specification. The LinkedIn Company Page logo is exported at **400 x 400 px**. Keep key text away from the edges because LinkedIn may crop cover imagery on different screens.
