Credential details

Evidence belongs to a specific entity and service.

Use RCC’s documentation contact to request the records relevant to your procurement. A technology provider’s credentials cover the services and boundaries identified in that provider’s documentation.

Entity registration

Request the exact legal entity name, Unique Entity ID (UEI), CAGE code, current SAM.gov status, and entity record link for the proposed contracting party.

Cloud services and FedRAMP

Request the offering name, provider, current Marketplace designation and level or class, listing link, and the deployment components covered by that listing.

SOC 2 examinations

Request the examined entity, covered system, Type I or Type II report, date or reporting period, and applicable distribution conditions.

Other certifications and affiliations

Request the issuer, credential or membership identifier, covered entity, scope, validity dates, and verification record relevant to the engagement.

Security & data handling

Match the review to the proposed environment.

Security questions depend on the service, data, and deployment involved. Request service-specific documentation before sharing sensitive information or approving a production environment.

Discuss customer responsibilities alongside RCC, hosting-provider, and subcontractor responsibilities.

Documentation to discuss

  • Access controls and multifactor authentication
  • Encryption and key responsibilities
  • Monitoring and incident reporting
  • Backups and recovery arrangements
  • Retention and deletion
  • Hosting locations and subprocessors
  • Shared responsibilities and contract requirements
Responsible AI

Ask how the proposed AI workflow operates.

AI behavior and data handling depend on the selected model, configuration, connected tools, and permissions. Discuss these decisions as part of the scope for an AI engagement.

For RCC’s AI Avatar & Agent Solutions, view the product development overview.

Questions for an AI engagement

  • Which actions require human review or approval?
  • How will outputs be evaluated and verified?
  • What data reaches model providers, and under what training and retention terms?
  • Which tools and systems can agents access?
  • What is logged, and who can review it?
  • How are errors handled and escalated?
